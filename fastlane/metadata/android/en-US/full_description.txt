Meet pagomi. — your everyday PDF scanner and editor.

Turn paperwork into clean, searchable PDFs. Capture documents with your camera, straighten pages, crop and enhance scans.

Everything your documents need:
• Scan receipts, notes, forms, books and IDs.
• Turn scans into searchable PDFs with on-device OCR.
• Merge, split, rotate, arrange and compress PDFs.
• Add signatures, watermarks and page numbers.
• Redact sensitive content and protect PDFs with passwords.
• Browse documents with Android's system file picker.
• Organize, export and share your saved documents.
• Optionally synchronize PDFs with your own Google Drive.

Designed for clarity. Core scanning and PDF tools work on your device; optional Drive sync needs an internet connection. No mandatory sign-up, ads or tracking.

Mathematical problem solving is not included in the current version.
