# pagomi. — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 23, 2026**

pagomi. – PDF Scanner & Editor is designed to scan, edit and organize documents on your Android device without mandatory registration.

## Your documents
The document scanner, PDF editor and text recognition operate on your device. The developer does not run a document-processing server, and does not receive your scanned pages, PDFs or extracted text. Documents you save reside in app-specific storage. They leave your phone when you choose to export or share them, or when you opt in to Google Drive synchronization.

## Optional Google Drive
If you enable Google Drive sync, pagomi. obtains authorization from the Google account you select and exchanges files directly with Google. The application stores your selected email address and synchronization metadata locally to maintain this connection. You can disconnect from Settings. Google processes data in Drive under Google's own privacy policy.

## Permissions
Camera access is used for capturing documents. The Android system file picker lets you select individual files without granting all-files access. Internet access supports optional Google authorization and Drive synchronization.

## Accounts, advertising and analytics
No developer-hosted account is required. This version does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs. The developer does not sell document data. If you submit a GitHub support issue, information you share there is handled by GitHub.

## Removing data
You can remove saved documents from the Files or Settings screens. Uninstalling the app removes its app-specific files, but not copies independently shared, exported, or synchronized to Google Drive.

## Changes and contact
Changes to this policy will be published here. For support or privacy inquiries: https://github.com/bren-wp/pagomi/issues
